---
title: Process Visualisation
description: How workflow diagrams represent a process.
---

A workflow diagram shows the steps and connections in an authored
[process](/docs/concepts/processes/). It helps you see how work moves between
participants and where the process can take different paths.

## Reading a workflow diagram

- **Step cards** represent the work defined in the process.
- **Flow lines** connect steps directly. Follow them to see which steps are
  connected, including alternate branches and exception paths.
- **Swimlanes** group steps by responsibility, including a system lane for
  automated work. A lane can also represent an anonymous participant.

The diagram represents the process definition. A particular execution follows
the paths determined by its conditions, data, and completed work; it does not
necessarily visit every step shown.

See [Process flow](/docs/concepts/process-flow/) for how connections and
conditions determine what happens next.
