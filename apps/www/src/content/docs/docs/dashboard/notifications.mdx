title: Notifications
description: Turn on desktop notifications for new To-Dos in the Dashboard.
---

The Dashboard can show desktop notifications when new
[To-Dos](/docs/dashboard/todos/) appear while it is open. Click a
notification to open the To-Do.

## Turn desktop notifications on or off

Click the bell in the Dashboard header to turn on desktop notifications.
If your browser asks for permission, allow notifications for the site.
Click the bell again to turn them off.

The preference applies to your account and organisation in the current
browser. If the browser blocks notifications, change the site's permissions
in the browser settings. The bell is hidden in browsers that don't support
desktop notifications.

The Dashboard doesn't notify you about To-Dos that were already there when
it loaded, or about new To-Dos while it is closed. Open **My To-Dos** to see
what's waiting for you.

For email settings, see the [notifications reference](/docs/reference/notifications/).
