---
title: Process execution
description: Start a process, follow its progress, and find work in the Dashboard.
---

Open **Executions** in the Dashboard to follow current or past work. A
**process execution** is one run of a [process](/docs/concepts/processes/),
such as a single order. Each execution has its own data and progress.

## Start and follow an execution

Use **Processes** to find and start a process. Once it has started, open the
execution in **Executions** to see its timeline and process state. The timeline
shows what happened in that run; the [workflow diagram](/docs/dashboard/visualisation/)
shows the paths the process can take.

## Find work you can complete

Open [**My To-Dos**](/docs/dashboard/todos/) to find work you can complete. Automated steps run without
waiting for someone to submit a form, and scheduled steps may wait until a
later time.

The actions available on an execution depend on your permissions. They can
include cancelling or restarting work.
