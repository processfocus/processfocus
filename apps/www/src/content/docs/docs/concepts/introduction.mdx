---
title: Introduction
description: How organisations, roles, and processes fit together in Process Focus.
---

In Process Focus, the organisation is where work belongs, roles describe who
is responsible for it, and processes define how it gets done.

- An [organisation](/docs/concepts/organisation/) represents the business or
  team you are modelling. Add organisational units, such as departments or
  teams, if you need them.
- A [role](/docs/concepts/roles/) represents a responsibility. Assign roles
  to users according to the work they do. Several users can share a role, and
  one user can hold several roles.
- A [process](/docs/concepts/processes/) defines the steps for a piece of work,
  what happens next, and which roles are responsible for its human steps.

For example, a company can put its reimbursement process in the Accounts
department. The process lets someone with the **Employee** role submit a
claim, then routes it to a step for someone with the **Accounts Manager**
role to review. Each claim runs through the same process as a separate
execution.
