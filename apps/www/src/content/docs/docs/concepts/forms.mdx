---
title: Forms
description: Collect and validate information with authored forms and business rules.
---

Use a **Form** to collect information in a
[process step](/docs/concepts/steps/) or to create and edit records in a
[List](/docs/concepts/lists/).

Employees can fill in forms, and anonymous users can submit forms such
as website enquiries. A process can also collect information from a
specific external recipient through a unique link.

## Fields and values

Fields define what values a form accepts and whether they are required,
optional, or read-only. An optional field can be left blank; its
definition still belongs to the form. Text, links, and containers can
help people fill in the form without collecting values themselves.

## Validation

Forms are partly validated when being filled in to make the data entry
process more pleasant.

On submission, the server validates the entire form and re-evaluates
its rules rather than trusting the browser's field state.

## Submitted forms

A submitted form becomes part of the process state. For every process
execution all submitted state is part of the single process state of
that execution, and can be referenced in subsequent steps.

## Form rules

A **Form Rule** derives field behaviour from the current input. Rules can hide
fields, disable editing, or make an optional field required.

For example, a form could ask whether an order needs delivery and require a
delivery address only when the answer is yes. The rule belongs to that form
and is checked when the form is submitted.

- A hidden field is not submitted. The browser may keep its draft value
  so it can restore what the person typed if the field reappears.
- A disabled field keeps its original or default value. The server
  ignores submitted edits to it.

## Embedded forms

[Embedded forms](/docs/reference/process-steps/embedded-forms/) let
visitors start a process from an external site, for example by
submitting a support ticket or enquiry.

## Public page forms

[Public page forms](/docs/reference/process-steps/public-page-forms/)
let visitors start a process from a public page or let a recipient
complete an existing step using a unique link.
