Use Model Context Protocol (MCP) to work with your Process Focus
organisation from an AI chat client.

## Chatting to your Process Focus system

[Connect an MCP client](/docs/reference/mcp/) to start processes or
complete To-Dos from your chat.

You can ask it questions such as “What To-Dos can I act on?” or “Where
is this order up to?” It can also browse [Lists](/docs/concepts/lists/) you can access.

## Permissions

When you connect a client, you choose **View work** or **View and act**.
**View and act** also lets the client start processes, complete To-Dos, or
change List records at your request. Process Focus still checks your
organisation permissions for each action. If a form needs input the client
cannot collect, you can finish it in the Dashboard.

![Process Focus consent screen for OpenCode with View work selected and View and act available.](/images/docs/mcp-opencode-consent.webp)

## Connections

You can connect your agent to multiple Process Focus environments (if
deployed via the Process Focus Cloud) or multiple Process Focus
organisations.
