An **Organisation** in Process Focus represents your company, department, or team—the top-level entity that contains all your processes, users, and configurations.

## The Organisation Hierarchy

The top level organisation can contain organisational units. This
allows you to model an organisation with departments, or a company
with teams.

Process Focus uses a hierarchical model to represent your organisation:

```
Organisation
├── Org Units
│   ├── Sales Department
│   ├── Engineering Team
│   └── Finance Department
├── Teams
│   └── Members (Users)
└── Roles
    └── Permissions
```

## Organisational Units (Org Units)

Org Units let you model the structure of your organisation. They are hierarchical containers that can represent:

- **Departments** - Sales, Engineering, HR, Finance
- **Teams** - Product Team A, Support Team B
- **Divisions** - Regional offices, business units
- **Projects** - Temporary structures for specific initiatives

Each org unit can have its own:

- Members and managers
- Processes and workflows
- Data visibility rules
- Approval hierarchies

> **Note:** For detailed information about creating and configuring org units, see the [Organisational Units](/docs/org-units) section.

## Organisation Settings

Organisation settings include:

- **Display Name** - How the organisation appears in the UI
- **Timezone** - Default timezone for dates and scheduling
- **Default Language** - Language for generated forms and notifications
- **Security Policies** - Password requirements, session timeouts
- **Branding** - Custom colors and logos (Enterprise plans)

## Best Practices

- Align org units with your actual business structure
- Use meaningful names that everyone understands
- Define clear ownership and responsibilities per org unit
- Regularly review and update the structure as your organisation evolves
