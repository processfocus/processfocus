An **Organisation** in Process Focus represents your company, department, or team—the top-level entity that contains all your processes, users, and configurations.

## The Organisation Hierarchy

Model the way your organisation is structured. Departments and teams are both
organisational units. You can place them directly under the organisation or
nest them to reflect how they fit together.

### Departments

If your organisation is divided into departments, place each department
directly under the organisation. For example, a manufacturer might have
Production, Sales, and Finance departments:

```text
Manufacturer
├── Production
├── Sales
└── Finance
```

### Teams without departments

If you have teams but no departments, place the teams directly under the
organisation. For example, a design studio might have a Design team and a
Client Services team:

```text
Design studio
├── Design
└── Client Services
```

### Teams within departments

If a department is divided into teams, place each team under its department.
For example, the manufacturer can split Production into Assembly and Quality
Control while leaving Sales and Finance as they are:

```text
Manufacturer
├── Production
│   ├── Assembly
│   └── Quality Control
├── Sales
└── Finance
```

## Best Practices

- Align organisational units with your actual business structure
- Use meaningful names that everyone understands
- Define clear ownership and responsibilities for each organisational unit
