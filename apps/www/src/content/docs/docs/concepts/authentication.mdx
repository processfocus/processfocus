---
title: Authentication
description: Understanding authentication in Process Focus.
---

Authentication is verifying the identity of a user or system. Are you
who you say you are? Authorisation is deciding what an authenticated
user or system can do.

## Supported Authentication Methods

- **Single-Sign-On**: recommended for businesses. Connect your user
  directory such as Google Workspace, or Microsoft Entra (Active
  Directory). This has important benefits: you do not have to provision
  users in Process Focus, and if an employee leaves, they will lose
  access to your Process Focus instance as soon as their identity is
  deactivated.
- **Passkeys**: use if you do not have a central user directory. Passkeys in Process Focus are easier than passwords, and passkeys are resistant to phishing.
- **API Secrets**: For service-to-service communication.

Process Focus does not support email-and-password authentication.

## Identity providers

We use the term identity provider for a service that provides
identities. Google Workspace and Entra are examples of such providers.

Any OAuth2 service should work with Process Focus.
