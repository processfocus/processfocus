---
title: SLAs and KPIs
description: Set time targets for work and measure how often you meet them.
---

A **service-level agreement (SLA)** sets a time target for a process or step.
A **key performance indicator (KPI)** measures performance over time. For
example, shipping an order within eight business hours is an SLA target.
The percentage of orders that met that target last month is a KPI.

## Set an SLA for a process or step

Use a process SLA when the target applies to an entire run of a
[process](/docs/concepts/processes/). Use a step SLA when the
target applies to one part of the work, such as approving an order within
two business hours. The step that starts a process cannot have its own SLA.

See the [SLAs reference](/docs/reference/processes/slas/) for configuration
examples.

Business-hour targets follow the organisation’s
[business calendar](/docs/reference/organisation-structure/settings/#business-calendar).
For example, if your working hours are 09:00 to 17:00 on weekdays, an
eight-business-hour target for an order received at 16:00 on Friday falls at
16:00 on Monday, assuming neither day is a holiday.

## Measure performance with a KPI

Choose a measure that answers a question about the process. To see how
consistently orders ship on time, divide the number of orders shipped within
the SLA by the total number of orders shipped in the same reporting period.
That measure describes the group of orders; the SLA is the target each
individual order is measured against.
